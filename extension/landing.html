Why teams love it

🗣️

Plain words in, work out

"sell 2 notebooks cash", "pay supplier bill BIL506 in cash", "create coupon Diwali10 amount 100" — full transactions from one sentence, in English or 8 Indian languages by voice.

🔒

No API key, no cloud

A local language engine — phrase rules, a typo-tolerant vocabulary and a meaning classifier — understands the command right in your browser. For a wording it has never seen, Chrome's own on-device AI reads it, and Vasy asks before acting on that reading. Nothing leaves the computer.

📚

Learns your business

Once a wording works, it is remembered and answered instantly next time; similar wordings are recognised too. Typo "oshwal shinde" once and it remembers the real supplier. Tab completes commands from what you have typed before.

📊

Live answers, zero navigation

"gross profit last quarter" drives the dashboard's own date picker and reads fresh numbers — with a "live as of" timestamp. Missing data? It opens the right report by itself.

🤝

Asks, never guesses

Two suppliers with similar names? You get tap-buttons to choose — with the rest of your command remembered. Wrong product? Send just the corrected name; it retries everything else.

🛡️

Data-integrity guard

If any detail you specified can't be placed, it refuses to save a half-filled record and tells you exactly what to check. No silent surprises in your books.

FAQ

Do I need an API key or an AI subscription?

No. Since v17 there is no AI service at all — every command is understood by the agent's own engine inside your browser. Nothing to sign up for, no quota, no credits.

What is the on-device AI, and how does Vasy learn?

Chrome has a small language model built in that runs on your own computer. Vasy uses it only for wordings its rules don't know: it rewrites your sentence into a command, shows you how it read it, and asks before doing anything that writes to your books. When a task built from a new wording succeeds, Vasy remembers that wording and answers it instantly next time — no model needed. Turn it on or off, or clear what was learned, from the Vasy toolbar menu or ⚙️ Settings. The toolbar menu also lists every remembered wording, so you can forget a single one that was learned wrong. Computers that can't run Chrome's model simply don't use it.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere unexpected. Everything — chat history and learned aliases — stays in your browser's local storage. Page reading happens in your tab, and the agent talks only to your own VasyERP server.

The product/supplier name I typed isn't exactly what's in the ERP — will it work?

Almost always. The fuzzy engine handles joined/split words ("mousepad" vs "mouse pad"), scrambled order, and small typos. When several records are close, you get tap-buttons to pick instead of a wrong guess — and your choice is remembered as an alias for next time.

Why did it refuse my message?

A strict scope gate politely turns away anything that isn't VasyERP business — jokes, weather, general chat. Mid-task answers like "5" or a name always pass.

What does the 🌐 badge on a reply mean?

You wrote or spoke in another language: Chrome's on-device translator turned it into English for the agent, and the reply was translated back to your language.

Something failed mid-way — do I start over?

No. On recoverable errors the agent keeps the task context and asks only for the broken piece — send the corrected product name or a different mobile number and it retries the rest automatically. It also never saves a half-filled record.

Which languages does voice support?

English (India/US), हिन्दी, ગુજરાતી, मराठी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, বাংলা and ಕನ್ನಡ — pick in ⚙️ Settings. With 🔊 on, results are spoken back in the same language.

Does it work on the new React pages of VasyERP?

Yes — field lookups fall back from IDs to placeholders to visible labels, and timing races with late-initialising components are waited out, so redesigned pages keep working.

How do I install it for my team?

Until the Chrome Web Store listing is live: chrome://extensions → enable Developer mode → Load unpacked → select the extension folder. Open VasyERP and press Alt+V.
